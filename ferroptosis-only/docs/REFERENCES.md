# Ferroptosis-Only Reference Library

This bibliography supports the mechanistic gene curation in `config/ferroptosis_master_gene_universe.csv`.

## Primary mechanistic references

### R01 — Foundational definition / iron / System Xc-
**Dixon SJ, Lemberg KM, Lamprecht MR, et al.** Ferroptosis: an iron-dependent form of nonapoptotic cell death. *Cell*. 2012;149(5):1060–1072.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2012.03.042  
PMID: 22632970  
Use: foundational ferroptosis definition; iron dependence; System Xc−/cystine biology; early genetic determinants including IREB2.

### R02 — GPX4
**Yang WS, SriRamaratnam R, Welsch ME, et al.** Regulation of ferroptotic cancer cell death by GPX4. *Cell*. 2014;156(1–2):317–331.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.12.010  
PMID: 24439385  
Use: GPX4 as central ferroptosis defense; glutathione depletion and GPX4 inhibition.

### R03 — System Xc− / SLC7A11 / CHAC1 response
**Dixon SJ, Patel DN, Welsch M, et al.** Pharmacological inhibition of cystine–glutamate exchange induces endoplasmic reticulum stress and ferroptosis. *eLife*. 2014;3:e02523.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.02523  
PMID: 24844246  
Use: erastin inhibition of System Xc−; SLC7A11 biology; CHAC1 as pharmacodynamic cystine-starvation response.

### R04 — Ferritinophagy / NCOA4
**Gao M, Monian P, Pan Q, et al.** Ferroptosis is an autophagic cell death process. *Cell Research*. 2016;26:1021–1032.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/cr.2016.95  
Use: NCOA4-mediated ferritinophagy, ferritin turnover and labile iron in ferroptosis.

### R05 — ACSL4/LPCAT3 genetics
**Dixon SJ, Winter GE, Musavi LS, et al.** Human haploid cell genetics reveals roles for lipid metabolism genes in nonapoptotic cell death. *ACS Chemical Biology*. 2015;10:1604–1609.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acschembio.5b00245  
PMID: 25965523

### R06 — ACSL4 mechanistic validation

## Curation policy

- Primary mechanistic papers override database labels.
- FerrDb is used as a completeness check, not as a source of automatic model inclusion.
- A gene appearing in many ferroptosis papers is not automatically a CORE gene.
- CORE requires strong causal/mechanistic evidence and reasonable RNA interpretability.
- Context-dependent or post-translational nodes remain EXTENDED/MECHANISTIC/PENDING until empirical calibration.
